Featured Project Experience

Real-World AI Automation Project

Student Scholarship AI Chatbot

AI Agents + RAG + Automation + APIs + Webhooks + Lead Qualification + Customer Classification + Human Escalation

This project goes beyond a traditional chatbot by connecting AI-powered conversations with automated business processes and human intervention when necessary.

Overview

An AI-powered customer support and lead qualification automation system designed for a company specialized in student travel and scholarship opportunities. The system automates communication with students, answers questions using a knowledge base, identifies potential travel leads, collects lead information, and escalates unknown questions to a human when necessary.

Project Status / Context

The project was initially presented to a company based on a real business requirement. The company approved the concept and requested that the system be implemented so they could evaluate the final solution.

Project Problem

The company was receiving a significant number of inquiries from students interested in studying abroad, scholarships, and travel opportunities. Handling these inquiries manually required significant time and could result in delayed responses. Delayed responses can cause potential students to lose interest or move to alternative providers. The business needed an automated system capable of responding quickly, providing relevant information, identifying serious potential customers, and reducing repetitive workload for the team.

Project Solution

I designed and built an AI automation workflow that receives incoming customer messages, analyzes their intent, retrieves relevant information from a knowledge base, and automatically determines the appropriate next action.

AI Agent Capabilities

01
Knowledge-Based Responses

Searches the knowledge base and retrieves relevant information before responding, providing answers based on company data rather than generic AI responses.

02
Lead Qualification

Analyzes intent to determine if the conversation represents genuine interest in studying or traveling, identifying qualified leads for follow-up.

03
Customer Classification

Analyzes incoming conversations and classifies customers, allowing different conversation types to follow the appropriate automation workflow.

04
Automated Lead Collection

Collects relevant information (name, phone, field of study, preferred country) when genuine interest is demonstrated, forwarding it to the responsible person.

05
Automated Notifications

Sends qualified leads automatically to the business owner or responsible team member through Email or Telegram.

06
Human Escalation

Escalates the conversation to a human via Email or Telegram when the AI lacks reliable information, avoiding uncertain responses.
